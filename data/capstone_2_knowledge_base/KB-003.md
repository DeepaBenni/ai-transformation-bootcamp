If the laptop shows no lights at all, perform a hard reset: disconnect power, hold the power button for thirty seconds, reconnect and retry. For Dell Latitude models the battery must be disconnected via the service hatch for this to take effect. If a single amber light blinks in a repeating pattern, count the blinks - two indicates a memory fault, three indicates a mainboard fault. Memory faults are usually resolved by reseating the DIMM. Mainboard faults require a depot repair and the user should be issued a loan device immediately from the local stock cupboard on their floor, with no approval required.
