---
id: KB-030
title: "Scanner sends documents to the wrong folder"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Scan-to-folder destinations are configured per user in the device address book and are not updated automatically when a user changes department. A scan arriving in an old team's folder indicates a stale address-book entry on that specific device, not a fault in the scanning software. Update the entry on the device itself; the change does not replicate to other devices, so users who scan from multiple machines need each one updated. Scan-to-email is unaffected by this and is a reasonable interim workaround.
