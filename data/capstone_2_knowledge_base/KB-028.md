---
id: KB-028
title: "Licence error when opening a design application"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Concurrent-licence applications release a seat only after a clean exit. Users who close the laptop lid without quitting hold their seat until the licence server times them out after two hours. Check the licence server console for the current seat holders before assuming the pool is exhausted. Seats can be reclaimed manually from the console. If the pool is genuinely at capacity during normal hours, this is a capacity request for the application owner, not a fault, and should be routed accordingly rather than resolved by reclaiming another user's seat.
