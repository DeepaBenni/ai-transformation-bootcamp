---
id: KB-015
title: "Database query timeouts during month-end"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Timeouts concentrated at month-end are caused by contention with the finance batch reconciliation job rather than by a query defect. The reconciliation job holds long-running locks on the ledger tables between 18:00 and 23:00 on the last two working days of each month. Reporting queries against those tables should be scheduled outside that window. If a query must run during it, the read-only replica is available and is not affected by the batch locks. Raising the client timeout value does not fix the contention and simply defers the failure.
