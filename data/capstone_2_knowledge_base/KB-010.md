---
id: KB-010
title: "Application crashes on login with error APP-771"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Error APP-771 is raised by the internal ERP client when the user's cached profile references a cost centre that no longer exists. This happens after a departmental reorganisation. Clearing the local cache directory under AppData\Local\ERPClient forces a fresh profile download on next login and resolves the crash. If the crash recurs after clearing the cache, the user's record in the ERP directory still points at the deleted cost centre and must be corrected by the Finance systems team. APP-771 is never a client installation fault, so reinstalling the application will not help.
