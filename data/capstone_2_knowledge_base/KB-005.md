---
id: KB-005
title: "Printer shows offline but responds to ping"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

A printer that answers ping but reports offline in Windows has usually lost its print spooler registration rather than network connectivity. Restart the Print Spooler service on the print server, not on the client. If the printer is deployed by Group Policy, confirm the queue still exists on the server; a deleted queue leaves a stale client-side entry that appears offline forever. For direct-IP printers, check the port configuration matches the printer's current address - printers that pick up a new DHCP lease are the single most common cause of this symptom.
