title: "Runbook: Password reset for a locked account"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: Verify the caller's identity using two pieces of information from the HR record - never accept a manager's word alone. Step 2: Confirm the account is locked rather than disabled; a disabled account indicates a leaver process and must not be re-enabled by the Service Desk. Step 3: Unlock the account in the directory console. Step 4: Reset the password only if the user cannot recall it; an unlock alone is sufficient after repeated failed logins. Step 5: Set the must-change-at-next-logon flag. Step 6: Record the verification method used in the ticket. Escalate to Identity Services if the account locks again within one hour, which indicates a cached credential on another device.
