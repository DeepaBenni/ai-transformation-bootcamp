title: "Runbook: Major incident declaration"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: A major incident is declared when a business-critical service is unavailable to multiple users with no workaround. Step 2: The Service Desk shift lead has authority to declare - do not wait for management approval. Step 3: Open the incident bridge and record the start time. Step 4: Appoint an incident manager who does not also perform technical work. Step 5: Send the first communication within fifteen minutes, even if it only states that the issue is being investigated. Step 6: Update stakeholders every thirty minutes regardless of whether progress has been made. Step 7: Do not close the bridge until the service is confirmed stable for thirty minutes.
