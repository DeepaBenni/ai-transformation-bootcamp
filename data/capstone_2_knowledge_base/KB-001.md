id: KB-001
title: "VPN disconnects every few minutes"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Users on the Cisco AnyConnect client may see the tunnel drop at regular intervals. The most common cause is an MTU mismatch after a wireless controller firmware update. Set the client MTU to 1300 on the affected profile. If drops persist, check the firewall for repeated SSL handshake failures from the client's public IP; more than five failures per minute indicates the session is being re-negotiated rather than kept alive. Escalate to the Network team if handshake failures continue after the MTU change. Do not reissue the user's certificate as a first step - certificate problems produce an immediate authentication failure, not periodic drops.
