title: "Runbook: Handling a data loss report"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: Stop the user from working in the affected location immediately - continued activity can overwrite recoverable data. Step 2: Establish what was lost, where it lived, and when it was last seen. Step 3: Check the recycle bin and version history before treating it as a restore request; most reported losses are recoverable this way. Step 4: If personal data may have been lost or exposed, notify the data protection officer within twenty-four hours; this is a legal obligation and is not discretionary. Step 5: Do not promise recovery to the user before it is confirmed. Step 6: Record the root cause even where data is recovered.
