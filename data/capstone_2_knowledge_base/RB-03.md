title: "Runbook: Offboarding a leaver"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: Disable the account on the leaving date, do not delete it. Step 2: Convert the mailbox to shared and grant the manager access for ninety days. Step 3: Revoke all active sessions and reset the password to a random value. Step 4: Reclaim licences after the mailbox conversion, not before, or the mailbox becomes inaccessible. Step 5: Collect the device and confirm the encryption key is still escrowed before re-imaging. Step 6: Transfer OneDrive content to the manager. Step 7: Retain the disabled account for ninety days before deletion. Any deviation requires HR approval recorded in the ticket.
