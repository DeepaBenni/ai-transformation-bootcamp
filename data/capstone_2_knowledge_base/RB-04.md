title: "Runbook: Responding to a suspected phishing report"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: Do not click any link in the reported message. Step 2: Confirm whether the user interacted with it - clicked, entered credentials, or opened an attachment. Step 3: If credentials were entered, immediately reset the password and revoke sessions before any further investigation. Step 4: Submit the message to the security team through the reporting add-in. Step 5: Search for other recipients using message trace and request a tenant-wide purge if the campaign is broad. Step 6: Do not reply to the sender or ask them to stop. Step 7: Thank the reporting user; discouraging reports is worse than a false positive.
