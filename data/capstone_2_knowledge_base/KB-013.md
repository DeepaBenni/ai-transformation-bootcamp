---
id: KB-013
title: "Remote desktop session disconnects after fifteen minutes"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

A fifteen-minute disconnect is the configured idle session timeout on the RDS collection and is working as designed. Sessions are disconnected, not logged off, so the user's work is preserved and reconnecting restores the session. If the user requires a longer window, a business justification is needed to move them to the extended-timeout collection. Disconnects at irregular intervals rather than exactly fifteen minutes indicate a network problem instead and should be investigated as such. Check the RDS gateway logs to distinguish an idle timeout from a transport-level drop.
