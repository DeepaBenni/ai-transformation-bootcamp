---
id: KB-008
title: "Password reset self-service portal rejects the new password"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

The self-service portal enforces the tenant password policy, which requires a minimum of twelve characters and blocks any password appearing in the banned-password list. It also blocks passwords that are too similar to the previous five. Users frequently hit the similarity rule without realising it, because the error message is generic. Advise a genuinely new passphrase rather than an incremented version of the old one. The portal does not enforce a character-class requirement, so a long lowercase passphrase is acceptable and is the recommended guidance for users who struggle with complexity rules.
