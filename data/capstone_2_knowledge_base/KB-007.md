---
id: KB-007
title: "Teams meeting audio drops for one participant"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Audio dropping for a single participant while others are unaffected is a client-side or last-mile issue, not a service outage. Have the user switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection to confirm. Check the Teams call analytics in the admin centre: round-trip times above 300ms or packet loss above 2% will produce this symptom. If the user is on VPN, split tunnelling should be enabled for Teams media traffic - routing media through the VPN concentrator is a frequent cause. Headset firmware is a less common but real cause on Jabra and Poly devices.
