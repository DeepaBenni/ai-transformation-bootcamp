---
id: KB-011
title: "Wi-Fi drops when moving between floors"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Roaming failures between access points are caused by the client holding on to a weak signal rather than by AP coverage gaps. Confirm the client supports 802.11r fast transition; older USB adapters do not and will always roam poorly. Set the roaming aggressiveness on Intel adapters to medium-high. Coverage should be verified with a survey before assuming a client fault, but a single user affected while colleagues on the same floor are fine points firmly at the client. Disabling the 2.4GHz band on the client profile improves roaming in buildings with dense 5GHz coverage.
