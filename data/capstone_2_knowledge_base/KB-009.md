---
id: KB-009
title: "Email delivery delayed by up to two hours"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Delivery delays of this length are typically caused by the message being held in the anti-malware sandbox for detonation. Attachments in Office format with embedded macros routinely take 20 to 90 minutes. Check the message trace in the security portal; a status of Pending with an ATP event confirms this. Delays affecting all mail regardless of attachment indicate a connector or queue problem and should be escalated immediately. Do not release messages from quarantine to bypass the delay without a documented business justification and approval from Information Security.
