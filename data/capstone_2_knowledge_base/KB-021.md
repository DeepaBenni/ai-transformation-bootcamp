---
id: KB-021
title: "Antivirus quarantines a legitimate business file"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

False positive quarantines should be released only after confirming the file's origin. Files downloaded from the internet or received by email must not be released without a security review, regardless of how urgently the user needs them. Internally produced files with a known author can be released by the Service Desk and an exclusion requested. Never add a folder-level exclusion to resolve a single-file detection; folder exclusions are a standing risk and require Information Security approval with a documented expiry date.
