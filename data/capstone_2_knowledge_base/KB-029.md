---
id: KB-029
title: "SharePoint site permissions not inherited by a subsite"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

A subsite created with unique permissions does not inherit later changes made to the parent. This is by design and cannot be retro-fitted without re-enabling inheritance, which discards the subsite's current permission set. Confirm the intended model with the site owner before changing anything. Permission changes take up to fifteen minutes to propagate to the search index, so a user who still cannot find content shortly after a change should be asked to retry rather than having their permissions changed again.
