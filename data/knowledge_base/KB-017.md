id: KB-017
title: "Mobile device shows 'device not compliant'"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

A non-compliant status blocks access to corporate resources until the device meets policy. The three most common causes are an out-of-date OS version, a disabled device passcode, and a rooted or jailbroken device. The compliance portal shows the specific failing rule. OS version failures resolve themselves once the user updates and the device checks in, which can take up to eight hours. Force a check-in from the Company Portal app to speed this up. Rooted devices cannot be made compliant and must be replaced; there is no exception process for this rule.
