---
id: KB-018
title: "Browser reports certificate error on internal sites"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Certificate errors on internal sites usually mean the device is missing the internal root CA certificate, which is deployed by Group Policy. Devices that have been off the network for an extended period may have missed the deployment. Force a policy refresh with gpupdate. If the error names an expired certificate rather than an untrusted issuer, the site certificate itself has lapsed and the application owner must renew it. Never advise users to click through a certificate warning as a workaround - this trains exactly the behaviour that phishing campaigns exploit.
