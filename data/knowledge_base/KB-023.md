id: KB-023
title: "User reports missing emails from a specific sender"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Check the message trace before assuming deletion. Mail from a specific sender that never arrives is most often blocked at the connector by an anti-spoofing rule when the sending domain has a strict DMARC policy and is being relayed by a third party. The trace will show a reject with a DMARC failure reason. Adding the sender to the safe-list does not override DMARC enforcement. The correct fix is for the sending organisation to authorise its relay in SPF, and the user should be told that this is not something the Service Desk can resolve unilaterally.
