title: "Runbook: Escalating to third-party vendor support"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: Confirm the issue is within the vendor's scope and the support contract is current. Step 2: Gather logs, exact error text, and the reproduction steps before opening the case - incomplete cases are the main cause of slow vendor response. Step 3: Open the case at the correct severity; inflating severity damages credibility and is noticed. Step 4: Record the vendor case number in the ticket. Step 5: Chase according to the contracted response time, not sooner. Step 6: Keep the internal ticket open and updated; never close it while a vendor case is live, as this loses the audit trail.
