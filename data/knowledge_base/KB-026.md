---
id: KB-026
title: "Guest Wi-Fi voucher not working"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Guest vouchers expire twenty-four hours after issue, not twenty-four hours after first use, which is the most common misunderstanding. A voucher issued the previous day will not work regardless of whether it was used. Vouchers are also single-device by default; a guest connecting a second device will be rejected with the same generic error. Multi-device vouchers can be issued on request by reception. The guest network is isolated from corporate resources, so a guest who needs access to internal systems requires a sponsored account instead.
