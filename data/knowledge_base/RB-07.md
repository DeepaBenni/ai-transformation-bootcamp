title: "Runbook: Emergency change during an incident"
type: runbook
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Step 1: Emergency changes bypass the normal advisory board but still require a record raised before implementation, not after. Step 2: Obtain verbal approval from the on-call change authority and record who gave it and when. Step 3: Document the backout plan before making the change - a change with no backout plan must not proceed under emergency rules. Step 4: Implement and verify. Step 5: Raise the retrospective documentation within one working day. Step 6: The change is reviewed at the next advisory board regardless of outcome. Skipping the retrospective record is treated as a compliance breach.
