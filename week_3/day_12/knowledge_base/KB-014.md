---
id: KB-014
title: "Software installation blocked by application control"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Installations blocked with a policy message are being denied by the application allow-list, not by a permissions problem. Granting local administrator rights will not bypass it and must not be used as a workaround. Submit the application for review through the software request catalogue; the security review takes three working days. Business-critical exceptions can be expedited by the Service Desk manager. Users who repeatedly attempt a blocked installation will generate security alerts, so advise them to stop attempting after the first denial and raise the request instead.
