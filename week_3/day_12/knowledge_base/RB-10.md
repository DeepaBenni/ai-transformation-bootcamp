Step 1: Confirm the fault is hardware and not a software failure; a device that posts but will not boot is a build problem, not a replacement case. Step 2: Local stock cupboards were closed in 2025 and no loan devices may be issued locally under any circumstances. Step 3: Raise a replacement request with Central Logistics, which ships a pre-built device within forty-eight hours. Step 4: Line-manager approval is mandatory for every replacement and must be recorded in the ticket before the request is raised. Step 5: Confirm the encryption key is escrowed before the failed device is collected. Step 6: The failed device must be returned to Central Logistics within five working days or it is charged to the department.
