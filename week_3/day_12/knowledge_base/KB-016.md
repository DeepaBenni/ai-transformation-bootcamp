---
id: KB-016
title: "New starter account not created on first day"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Account creation is driven by the HR feed, which runs nightly at 02:00. A starter whose HR record was completed after that run will not have an account until the following morning. This is expected behaviour, not a fault. Urgent same-day accounts require a manual creation request approved by the hiring manager. Check the HR record has a populated start date and cost centre; either field being blank causes the feed to skip the record silently, which is the most common cause of a genuinely missing account rather than a timing issue.
