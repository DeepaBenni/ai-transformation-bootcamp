---
id: KB-027
title: "Disk encryption recovery key not found"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

Recovery keys are escrowed to the device record in the management console at the point of encryption. A device with no key present was almost certainly encrypted before enrolment or was re-imaged without re-enrolling. Check the previous device record before concluding the key is lost. If no key exists anywhere, the data is unrecoverable and the device must be re-imaged; there is no vendor backdoor and no escalation path that recovers it. This is the reason the pre-enrolment encryption check exists in the build process and must not be skipped.
