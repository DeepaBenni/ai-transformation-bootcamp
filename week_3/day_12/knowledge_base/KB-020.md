---
id: KB-020
title: "Conference room display shows no signal"
type: article
status: current
last_reviewed: 2026-08-20
---

No-signal faults are most often a source selection problem rather than a hardware failure. Confirm the display is on the correct HDMI input; the room control panel resets to input one overnight. If the correct input is selected, test with a known-good cable, as the fixed cables in meeting rooms fail regularly from repeated connection cycles. USB-C connections that charge the laptop but show no picture indicate a cable that carries power but not video. Rooms with a dedicated Teams Room device should be restarted from the console rather than at the wall socket.
